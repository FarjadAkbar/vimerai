# Phase 1 offers Length Tiers; reels use four marketing beats

Users pick a Length Tier before Generate. Phase 1 tiers are Teaser (~8–10s, default, fast) and Promo (~60s, queued, higher cost). Promo Video is beat-aligned stitch in Phase 1; native long-form may replace stitch later. Creative Brief and Reel Storyboard always structure marketing reels as hook → attention → product display → viewer connection — not ad-buy creatives. Chosen over a single fixed short clip or a three-tier ladder so promo-length storytelling is first-class without bloating MVP options.
