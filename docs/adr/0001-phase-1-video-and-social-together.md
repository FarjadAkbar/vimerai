# Phase 1 ships video and social content together

Phase 1 is a single Generation flow that produces real short-form video plus companion social post / storyboard Content Outputs (B+A), rather than video-only or copy-only first. Chosen to match the product vision in one loop despite higher surface area and cost versus finishing the existing video path alone.

## Considered Options

- **B-first with thin captions** — faster, builds on fal path; deferred full social studio
- **A-first (posts + storyboards only)** — pauses the video capability already in the codebase
- **B+A together (chosen)** — one create action yields both video and social/storyboard outputs
